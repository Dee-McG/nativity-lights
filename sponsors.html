How payment works

  1. Reserve your animals here. Click the button to hold your animals and send us your details.
  2. Donate on iDonate. We’ll take you straight to our iDonate page and email you the link too. Donate the total there, and put your business name in the message so we can match it.
  3. You’re on the board. Once Tom sees your donation, your animals are marked as sponsored on this page. iDonate emails you a receipt for your records.

Animals are held for 24 hours while your donation comes through, so please donate straight away.
